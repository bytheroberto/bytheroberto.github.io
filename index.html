🎮 pocketvibe - Play Retro Games Made With AI

Play three.js games on your retro handheld, and make your own with AI. A launcher, game store and starter kit for ROCKNIX and Android handhelds.

⬇️ Download pocketvibe

Free and open source. Windows setup guide included below.

📖 What Is pocketvibe?

pocketvibe is a friendly little app that turns your retro handheld into a modern game machine. Instead of only playing old cartridges and classic emulators, it gives you a whole new world of games built with three.js, a popular technology for making fast, colourful 3D games that run right in a web browser.

Think of pocketvibe as three helpful things bundled into one download. First, it is a launcher — a tidy menu that sits on your device and starts your games with a single button press, no file hunting required. Second, it is a game store — a built-in catalogue where you can browse and grab new games whenever you like. Third, it is a starter kit — a set of ready-made building blocks that lets you (or an AI helper) create your very own game and add it to the same menu.

The whole project is open source, which means it is free to use, free to share, and always improving thanks to the people who use it. You do not need to know anything about programming to enjoy the games. If you do get curious about making your own, pocketvibe is designed to hold your hand through that too.

✨ What You Can Do With It

  • Browse a game store built for handhelds. New three.js games appear in one place, sorted and ready to play. No searching random websites, no sketchy downloads.
  • Launch everything from one menu. Your games live in a clean launcher that feels like a real console dashboard. Big buttons, easy navigation, controller friendly.
  • Play games made for small screens. Every game in the store is chosen with handheld screens and physical controls in mind, so text stays readable and buttons stay reachable.
  • Build your own game with AI. The starter kit gives you a working template. Describe the game you want to an AI assistant, and it can help you write the pieces. You stay in charge of the ideas.
  • Add your creation to your own launcher. Once your game works, drop it in and it shows up next to the store games like it always belonged there.
  • Keep everything offline friendly. Your device stays yours. Your games live on your handheld, not in someone else's cloud.
  • Use hardware you already own. pocketvibe targets ROCKNIX and Android handhelds, including popular H700-based devices from Anbernic and similar makers.

🚀 Getting Started on Windows

You can prepare everything you need from a normal Windows computer and then copy it across to your handheld. You will not need to install any developer tools.

⬇️ Open the pocketvibe download page

Visit this link to download the application.

Step by step

  1. Open the download page using the button above. It opens your normal web browser, so no special app is required.
  2. Download the latest release listed at the top of that page. The newest one is always the best choice, because it contains the most recent fixes.
  3. Save the file somewhere easy to find, such as your Downloads folder or your Desktop. Avoid burying it inside lots of subfolders.
  4. If you are asked whether you want to keep the file, choose to keep it. Windows sometimes asks this for files downloaded from the internet.
  5. Connect your handheld to your Windows computer using its USB cable. When the device appears in File Explorer, open it and look for the folder that holds your games.
  6. Copy the downloaded pocketvibe files across to that folder. A simple copy and paste is all that is needed — do not rename anything.
  7. Safely eject the handheld from Windows, unplug the cable, and restart the device.
  8. Launch pocketvibe from your device's menu. You should see the game store and launcher appear.

If your handheld does not appear on your computer, try a different USB cable first. Many cables are for charging only and cannot carry files. Using a cable that came with a phone or a camera usually works.

🕹️ Playing Your First Game

Once pocketvibe is running, you will land on the launcher screen. Use the directional pad or the analogue stick to move between items, and press the main action button to select. Everything is designed to be readable on a small screen, so the text will be large and the menus will be short.

Go to the store section to see what is available. Pick any game that catches your eye and choose to install it. A small progress bar will appear, and the game will join your library when it finishes. From then on, you can start it straight from the launcher without going through the store again.

If a game feels too quiet, too small, or too fast, most titles include their own settings screen. Look for a small gear or menu icon while the game is running. You can usually leave a game and return to the launcher by pressing the menu button on your device.

🤖 Making Your Own Game With AI

This is where pocketvibe becomes something more than a games console. The starter kit includes a small, working game project and clear notes that explain what each part does. You do not have to write everything from scratch — you can describe what you want in plain English and let an AI assistant help you produce the code.

A typical session looks like this. You open the starter kit files on your Windows computer. You tell your AI assistant something like "make the player move faster" or "add a score counter at the top of the screen". The assistant writes the changes, you copy the updated files to your handheld, and you test the result. Repeat until it feels right. The loop is quick, which makes learning surprisingly fun.

The starter kit is built on three.js, which means your finished game runs smoothly on modest hardware and does not need a large download. When you are happy with your creation, you can share it with the community so other people can play it on their own handhelds.

📱 Which Handhelds Does It Work With?

pocketvibe is made for two families of devices. The first is ROCKNIX, a lightweight operating system many hobbyists install on their retro handhelds. The second is Android, which covers a very wide range of devices from small pocket consoles to larger handheld tablets with built-in controls.

Devices built around the H700 chip are a particularly good match, including popular models from Anbernic. These machines have sharp little screens and proper buttons, which is exactly what pocketvibe is designed for. Games that rely on a keyboard and mouse will always feel a bit odd, so the store favours titles that work beautifully with a directional pad and two or three face buttons.

If your handheld already runs Android and has a web browser, there is a good chance pocketvibe will feel at home on it. The launcher is light on memory and light on storage, so it leaves plenty of room for your existing game collection.

🧰 What's Inside

  • The launcher. A clean home screen that gathers your installed games in one place and starts them quickly.
  • The game store. A browsable catalogue of three.js games chosen for handheld play, with one-press installs.
  • The starter kit. A working project template with simple explanations, ready to be shaped by you and your AI assistant.
  • Helper notes and examples. Short, plain-language guidance on adding games, adjusting settings, and getting the most out of the setup.

❓ Troubleshooting

The launcher does not appear after restarting. Double-check that the pocketvibe files ended up in the correct folder on your device, and that nothing was renamed during the copy. Then restart once more.

My computer cannot see the handheld. Try a different USB cable and a different USB port on your computer. Avoid USB hubs where possible. Charge-only cables are the single most common cause of this problem.

Everything feels slow. Close any apps you are not using and restart the device. Web-based games run best when they are the only thing in focus.
